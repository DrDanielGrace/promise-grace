One question, and the five areas underneath it.

Everything on this site comes out of one question, and the honest version of that question is that it is not one question. It is a chain: take gravity away and you take away buoyancy, take away buoyancy and you take away convection, and once transport is happening by diffusion alone, nucleation, growth, solidification and the structure that comes out of them are all different. Each link in that chain is somewhere she has built something.

The five areas

What is claimed, and what is not

COMPUTED
Every number on the readout comes out of the governing equations, evaluated live. The equation is shown at the maths depth and the function running it is one control away. Fourteen of the seventeen are this.
MEASURED
At least one quantity is integrated off a real table rather than a curve drawn to look right. The solar spectrum work uses the ASTM G173 reference spectrum, and the efficiency limit is detailed balance computed on that same table.
SPECULATIVE
A prediction with nothing behind it yet. There is one: what a stalactite does without gravity. It is dated, the box beside it is empty, and it will stay empty until she finds the paper or somebody sends it to her.
PROXY
Where a simulation hands a quantity to another simulation and that quantity is not derived, it is declared a proxy at both ends. The strain the crystal growth simulation sends to the diffraction simulation is the only one.

None of this is laboratory work and none of it is presented as laboratory work. It is a chemistry graduate building the instruments she would want to have, checking them against the literature where the literature exists, and saying so where it does not.
