The visa question, which decides the rest

A funded offer is only usable if it comes with a way for both of us to be in the country and for both of us to earn. That constraint is settled first, because it eliminates more programmes than any academic requirement does.

Austria Verified

The whole thing turns on one clause. ISTA PhD students hold a Settlement Permit – Researcher, and that permit is not restricted to people who already hold a doctorate:

The “Settlement Permit – Researcher” is intended for nationals of third countries who are in possession of a doctoral/PhD degree or a degree allowing access to doctoral/PhD programmes…

OeAD, Austrian Agency for Education and Internationalisation

A B.Sc.(Ed.) is a degree allowing access to a doctoral programme, and being admitted is the proof of it. The family members of that permit then receive a Red-White-Red Card Plus, which the Austrian government's migration portal describes as granting “unlimited labour market access (as a self-employed or an employed person, not limited to a specific employer)”.

The one open risk To confirm

Austria has two relevant permits and which one applies depends on status: researchers doing scientific work get the researcher permit, people enrolled in a doctoral programme get a student permit, and only the first carries the family work rights. ISTA's own Welcome Services page names the researcher route and ISTA employs its PhD students under contract, which is the trigger for it, so the evidence points the right way.

But a Bachelor's entrant spends roughly the first eighteen months on Master's-level coursework before the Qualifying Exam, and that phase is where the classification could go the other way. One email to ISTA's HR settles it, and it is the first thing on the list below.

United States Closed for a spouse

The F-2 spouse and children of an F-1 student may not accept employment.

8 CFR 214.2(f)(15)(i)

There is no application to file and no exception in the text. Funded US PhD students are almost always on F-1, which puts a spouse on F-2. A J-2 spouse of a J-1 exchange visitor may apply for employment authorization on Form I-765, but which category a department sponsors follows from how the student is funded, and a departmental assistantship usually means F-1.

A second problem arrived in July. A DHS final rule effective 15 September 2026 replaces duration of status with a fixed admission period capped at four years. A chemistry PhD runs five to six, so it would mean filing an extension of stay with USCIS mid-degree.

New Zealand Entry closed

The best spouse terms found anywhere: a Partner of a Student Work Visa lets the partner do “any work, in any job, anywhere in New Zealand” with no job offer needed, NZD 1,630, decided in about five weeks, and international PhD candidates pay domestic tuition.

And it is shut. Otago requires a Bachelor's with first or upper second class honours at a B+ average including a research component, or a Master's with one. This one reopens after a research Master's, and when it does it is arguably the best destination on the list.

Germany, Australia, Canada

Germany works on the same logic as Austria: a researcher permit “also applies to doctoral candidates if they are employed by an academic institution”, and a researcher's spouse may take any job with no German language requirement. Whether a given Max Planck school employs or stipends is a per-school question. Australia gives dependants unlimited work hours where the student is on a doctoral degree, but the visa costs from AUD 2,500 and the entry bar is probably New Zealand's. Canada is the route already in motion: a spousal open work permit where the programme is a master's of sixteen months or longer, or a PhD, at a designated learning institution.

Why the research fits

Chemistry & Materials is a named track with thirteen groups, and two of them sit directly on what she already works towards. Stefan Freunberger leads Materials Electrochemistry: new materials and reaction mechanisms in electrochemical energy storage, metal-oxygen and metal-sulfur batteries, electrocatalysis, studied with in-situ spectroscopy, diffraction and microscopy. Maria Ibáñez leads Functional Nanomaterials and runs the Werner Siemens Thermoelectric Laboratory on high-efficiency thermoelectric materials. Bartholomäus Pieber works on heterogeneous photocatalysis with semiconductors. Electrochemical materials, thermoelectrics and solar energy materials are three of the five things on her research list, and each is a group name rather than a stretch.

Crystal growth is not on the track page, and nothing at ISTA touches microgravity. That interest belongs in the statement of purpose, where nucleation, solidification and phase transitions connect to it honestly. It is not something to search on.

Wayne State University · Chemistry Spouse blocked

Detroit. Full funding at roughly USD 29,000 plus tuition and health insurance, twelve months a year, and the department states that all admitted PhD students receive support. Its published requirement is a “minimum of 3.0 or its equivalent in upper division coursework”, which is the criterion her upper-division record meets. English is the hurdle: TOEFL iBT 90 with every subsection at 20 or better, with no waiver mentioned. Deadline 1 December.

Tufts University · Chemistry Spouse blocked

Medford, Massachusetts. The department states plainly that “there are no specific requirements for grade point average”, and asks for “an undergraduate degree in chemistry, or in a related subject with appropriate chemistry courses”, which is the most accommodating wording found anywhere for a B.Sc.(Ed.) holder. Unilorin teaches in English, so the TOEFL is worth asking about before paying for it.

Max Planck Schools and IMPRS Autumn 2026

Direct-track and fast-track routes from a Bachelor's exist, and IMPRS-CMS at Stuttgart runs a bachelor fellowship. Not every school offers the route, so this is a list to work through when 2027 projects are announced, asking each one whether doctoral candidates are employed or stipended, because that is what decides the spouse position in Germany.

What happens next

Now

  1. Treat ISTA as the primary application. No fee, no TOEFL, no tuition. The only cost is the time to write it well.
  2. Ask ISTA HR, in writing, whether a student admitted with a Bachelor's is sponsored on the Settlement Permit – Researcher route from the start, or on a student permit during the coursework phase. Everything in the Austrian section rests on this.
  3. Write to Freunberger and Ibáñez about their actual work, not about the institute.
  4. Ask WES whether the evaluation states an upper-division GPA, since the trajectory argument is far stronger as their figure than as a reconstruction.

Within the month

  1. Start the Austrian documents. Police clearance must be under three months old at application, legalised and then translated, and so must the marriage certificate. These are slow from Nigeria and cannot be left to December.
  2. Begin German. Nothing is needed to arrive, but Integration Agreement module 1 is needed within two years, and doing it early removes a deadline from a busy second year.

In parallel

  1. Ask Tufts and Wayne State two questions: whether admitted international students are sponsored on F-1 or J-1, and whether a Unilorin medium-of-instruction letter is accepted in place of the TOEFL. The first decides whether the offer is usable; the second decides whether a test needs paying for.
  2. Work the IMPRS list when 2027 projects are announced in autumn.
