Promise Oluwatosin Grace

Chemistry graduate. Teaching secondary school chemistry in Lagos, including the practical work. Teaching herself materials science in the evenings, and building the instruments she wanted to have while she does it.

Who she is

A B.Sc. in Chemistry from the University of Ilorin, with a Biology minor, assessed by World Education Services. Two years teaching chemistry in Nigerian secondary schools, first on national service in Akwa Ibom and now in Lagos, which means lesson planning, marking, and running practicals with whatever the laboratory actually has in it.

The first two years of the degree were not good. Every year after that was better, the final year was the strongest, and the courses she did best in were the hardest ones: coordination chemistry, reaction kinetics, radio-nuclear chemistry, organometallics. The research project came out at the top of the range. She says all of this herself, plainly, in entry 09, which is a better place to read it than a summary of it here.

In one week in June 2026, teaching a full timetable, she worked through Arizona State's materials science specialisation end to end, then the University of London's research methods course. Every certificate is linked and every link is checkable.
