  1. 01

    The question

    the thing I keep coming back to

    Why a crystal grown in space comes out cleaner than one grown here. Nucleation, then growth with a gravity slider, then the X ray pattern that proves the difference is measurable rather than just visible. Three simulations, and each one hands its result to the next.

  2. 02

    Phase diagrams, properly explained

    this took me a while to really get

    A phase diagram is not a picture, it is a set of instructions. Pick a composition, press cool, and watch which microstructure you get. Dendrites at one composition, eutectic lamellae at another.

  3. 03

    A titration you can get wrong

    every student overshoots the first time

    The curve draws itself as the drops fall. Pick the wrong indicator for a weak acid and you will read the endpoint ninety one percent early and never know. That mistake is the lesson.
