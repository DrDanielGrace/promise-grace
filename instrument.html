    What this is

    Move gravity from 1 g down towards zero and the reason for the difference appears. Under gravity, convection keeps sweeping fresh solution past the crystal, so it grows fast and unevenly. Take gravity away and nothing stirs, so a depleted shell builds up around the crystal, growth slows, and because it is now limited by how fast solute can diffuse in, it becomes steady and even. The Péclet number is the ratio of the two kinds of transport, and the change happens as it passes one.
